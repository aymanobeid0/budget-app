## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: The parts that belong to this product are good: the month header with swipe, the "Spent in October" card with the comparison to the previous month, the breakdown sheet that ranks Categories and filters on tap, and the Net line. The rest of the frame is a stock fintech template: a blue hero card, tinted icon squares, an icon-only five-tab bar and a settings list of colored squares. The product's whole thesis is a three-second log, and that action has no signature surface. It is a 44px square in the top-left corner of two tabs, and it is missing from Home.

Deterministic scan: not applicable (native platform).

## Priority Issues

[P1] The three-second log is buried. Home, where the app opens, has no add action. Adding lives in a 44px header square at the top left of the Expenses and Income tabs, the hardest spot to reach with a thumb. On first launch, "Add your first Expense" sits below the fold under zero-value cards. The form then preselects the alphabetically first Category, so the fastest path files everything under Entertainment. Fix: a thumb-zone add action reachable from Home and the Expenses tab; default to the last-used Category (or none, with the amount focused); on first launch, put the add action above the zero cards. Command: /impeccable layout, then /impeccable harden for the Category default.

[P1] Home and the Expenses tab are the same screen twice. Both show the month header, a total card and this month's Expenses. Home adds a Today/This week strip on the blue card and a Net card with its own This week/This month/All time switch that ignores the month header. Two period systems on one screen, and a fifth tab that adds nothing. Fix: make Home the month overview (Spent and Net for the chosen month, each tapping through) and leave the list to the Expenses tab, or fold the Expenses tab into Home; have Net follow the chosen month. Command: /impeccable distill.

[P1] Icon-only navigation. Five unlabeled tabs, with Income and Expenses drawn as near-identical money glyphs, and inactive icons at about 2.2:1 contrast. Friends and family installing the app have to learn the bar by trial. Fix: labeled tabs following each OS (a Material 3 navigation bar on Android, a tab bar with labels on iOS), with the inactive color raised to at least 3:1. Command: /impeccable adapt.

[P2] Copy breaks the glossary and the voice. "Transactions" heads the Home list; Profile shows "Add your Supabase keys to .env to enable accounts"; a negative Net shows a red trending-down icon labelled "Behind", which grades spending against the Money Hues rule and the "read, don't grade" principle; "Clear Chat" uses the compose icon; lists can print raw "Error: <exception>". Command: /impeccable clarify.

[P2] Sibling screens disagree. The total card is blue and full width on Home but white, centered and shrink-wrapped on the Expenses and Income tabs. Expense rows swipe to delete; Income rows don't. Expense rows under a day header repeat the day ("Yesterday" / "Yesterday"). There are three dark-mode controls (Home header, Profile header, Profile switch). Back is a raised square on the form but a bare arrow on Categories and AI provider. The add/edit page keeps the tab bar live under a full-screen dialog. Currency and Income Sources share the same icon. Command: /impeccable polish.

## Persona Red Flags

Casey (distracted mobile user): opens on Home, finds no add action, switches to Expenses, stretches to the top-left +, types 450, taps Add Expense, and the expense lands in Entertainment because that chip was preselected. Five steps, one of them a corner reach, and a silent wrong Category.

Jordan (first-timer, a friend who installed it): first launch shows a blue card of zeros, a Net card of zeros and a This week/This month/All time switch before any explanation; the only action is below the fold. The tab bar has no words. Profile tells them to "Add your Supabase keys to .env".

Sam (accessibility): inactive tab icons at about 2.2:1; the switch's off track is nearly invisible on white; the Consultant's send button stays bright blue and glowing while it is disabled; the decorative sparkle square in the Consultant header looks like a button but does nothing.

## Minor Observations

- Profile says the same thing three times when offline: "This phone · Cloud backup is off", "Accounts unavailable" and "Not connected · kept on this phone only".
- The Consultant's Clear Chat stays enabled on an empty chat, and the off-state copy says "Add it in Settings" while the button goes straight to the AI provider screen.
- "Remove key" deletes a saved API key with no confirmation, unlike every other destructive action.
- Every AI provider row uses the same sparkle icon and blue, so the five providers only differ by name.
- The amount is parsed through a double before becoming minor units, which goes against the "Money is an int" convention.
- Shadows rendered as hard bands in the test renderer; judge real-device shadow quality separately.
- The Categories screen counts "this month" by the device clock, not the chosen month.

## Questions to Consider

- What if logging an Expense took one tap from anywhere: amount first, Category remembered?
- Does Home need a list at all, or is it the month at a glance with two doors (Expenses, Income)?
- Would four labeled tabs serve better than five unlabeled ones?
- Should a negative Net look different from a positive one at all, or just carry its sign?
